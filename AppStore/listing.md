## Description [4000 characters]

Turn your iPhone into a remote for your Samsung Smart TV. Free Remote finds TVs on your Wi-Fi network automatically and gives you the buttons you use every day, laid out like a classic remote.

FEATURES
- Finds Samsung TVs on your Wi-Fi automatically, or add one by its IP address
- Power on and off, volume, mute and channel up/down
- Circular D-pad with OK, plus Back and Home
- Source button, with a long press for HDMI 1 to 4
- Play/Pause and a number pad
- Type into search boxes on the TV with your iPhone keyboard
- Hold volume, channel or the arrows to repeat
- Remembers your TVs, even while they are asleep
- Give each TV your own name; names sync across your devices with iCloud
- Haptic feedback, VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Free Remote talks only to TVs on your local network. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking, and no data is collected. The connection to your TV is encrypted, and the app remembers your TV's security identity so it notices if something changes.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Free Remote is free, with no in-app purchases. Its source code is published on GitHub under the MIT license.

REQUIREMENTS
- Works with Samsung Smart TVs running Tizen, 2022 and newer. Many 2018–2021 models also work. Tested on 2022, 2024 and 2026 models.
- Your iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi/local network. Guest or isolated networks that block devices from seeing each other will not work.
- The first time you connect, choose "Allow" on the TV.
- To turn the TV on from the app, enable "Power On with Mobile" (network standby) in the TV's settings. If the TV has been off for a long time, it may need its own remote to turn on.
- To find the TV automatically, keep AirPlay turned on in the TV's settings (it is on by default), or add the TV by its IP address.
- Allow Local Network access on your iPhone when asked (Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network).
- Requires an iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
